## What Problem Does Mojaloop Solve?

Digital financial services can be fragmented.
Mobile wallet providers and traditional banks operate in closed-loop networks where customers of one provider cannot easily send money to customers of another without high fees or complex workarounds.

Mojaloop acts as an interoperability **switch** — a Central Hub that connects these disparate networks, enabling real-time, low-cost payments to flow seamlessly between providers.

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Interoperability** — Instant, 24/7 clearing and settlement across different financial institutions (banks-to-wallets, wallets-to-wallets, etc.)
- **Financial Inclusion** — Lowers the technical and financial barriers for smaller institutions to connect to a national payment rail
- **Open Source & Royalty-Free** — Licensed under Apache 2.0, preventing vendor lock-in and enabling free deployment and customization
- **Risk Management** — Supports Net Debit Caps and real-time liquidity validation before transactions are approved
- **Flexible Settlement** — Integrates with national RTGS systems or custom settlement mechanisms
- **Payment Initiation Service Integration** - Support integration with third-party expanding payment interoperability.
