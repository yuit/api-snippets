## How do I contribute?

- Read and familiarise yourself with our [product engineering processes](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/contributing/product-engineering-process.html) and [Mojaloop design and code review processes](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/contributing/design-review.html).
- Review the [Mojaloop Deployment Guide](https://docs.mojaloop.io/documentation/deployment-guide/) and the [Onboarding Guide](https://github.com/mojaloop/mojaloop/blob/master/onboarding.md).
- Browse through the [Repository Overview](https://docs.mojaloop.io/documentation/repositories/) to understand how the Mojaloop code is managed across multiple GitHub repositories.
- Get familiar with our [Standards](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/standards/guide.html) for contributing to this project.
- Go through the [New Contributor Checklist](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/contributing/new-contributor-checklist.html), and browse through the project board and work on your [good first issue](https://github.com/mojaloop/project/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22).
- Review the [Roadmap](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/mojaloop-roadmap.html) and contribute to future opportunities.
- Familiarize yourself with our Community [Code of Conduct](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/contributing/code-of-conduct.html).

## What work is needed?

Mojaloop follows a structured [product engineering process](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/contributing/product-engineering-process.html) and we actively maintain a [roadmap](https://docs.mojaloop.io/community/mojaloop-roadmap.html) of new feature developments and maintenance work. You can find information about our currently running official workstreams on our [community central workstreams page](https://community.mojaloop.io/pi-24-workstreams).

Each Mojaloop workstream maintains a work item backlog in GitHub and a ZenHub workspace. Reach out to the workstream lead or post a message on the workstream Slack channel to introduce yourself and find a good ticket to start work on. You will find contact details for the workstream leads and Slack channel information on our [community central workstreams page](https://community.mojaloop.io/pi-24-workstreams).
